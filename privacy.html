Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What is stored in your browser

SkinCare Guide currently stores planner settings, product selections, calendar progress, language and theme preferences, and your skin profile in your browser's local storage. This information stays on the device and browser you use. It is not currently synchronised to your account or uploaded to our database. Clearing your browser's site data may delete this information.

Account and authentication

If you choose to create an account or sign in, authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication, a service from Google. Depending on the sign-in method, Firebase may process your email address, account identifier, display name, and profile photo. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not stored in SkinCare Guide's browser storage. Your browser may retain an authentication session so you remain signed in until you sign out or the session is cleared. Google sign-in is optional; you can continue as a guest.

SkinCare Guide has not yet enabled cloud synchronisation or Firestore storage. Signing in does not currently transfer your guest planner data or skin profile into an online account.

You can read more about how Firebase handles data in the Firebase privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You may use the planner as a guest without creating an account. You can sign out of an account using the Sign out button. You can remove locally stored app data by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

Contact

A dedicated privacy contact email will be published here before the public launch. This policy should be reviewed and completed with the project owner's contact details before the service is released more widely.
