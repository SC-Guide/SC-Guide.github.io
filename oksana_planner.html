Important Precautions

  • Two retinols — NEVER in one night! Alternate The Ordinary Retinol 1% and La Roche-Posay Retinol B3 Serum on different evenings.
  • Glycolic acid — separate evening! Do not use with retinol or other acids. Max 1x per week to start.
  • SPF is mandatory every morning! Even on cloudy days. Reapply every 2 hours in strong sun.
  • Patch test new products — especially Medicube PDRN and CARE B Easy Sun.
  • Retinol only on DRY skin — applying to damp skin increases irritation risk.
  • Kiko SPF10 is NOT enough — use only on top of SPF50, never instead of it.
  • Do not combine The Ordinary Multi-Peptide + HA with direct acids, vitamin C, or salicylic acid in the same routine.
