💡 Idea & Concept

Skincare Guide was born from a simple yet critical need: helping people use active skincare ingredients safely and confidently. Whether you're starting retinal, exploring acids, or building a complex routine, the project guides you through every step — from product selection to daily tracking.

Problem Fear of active ingredients, lack of safety guidance, ingredient conflicts.
Solution A visual planner with safety-first rules, conflict detection, and step-by-step tracking.
Result A portfolio‑ready tool that combines safety education with engaging UX and scalable architecture.
✨ Key Features

Every feature was designed with a specific user need in mind — from safety-first guidance to persistent progress tracking.

🛡️ Safety-first Guidance
Conflict detection, warnings, and safe usage tips for retinal, acids, masks, and more.
🐍 Snake‑style Calendar
Days flow in a winding path (boustrophedon) — more engaging than a standard grid.
🌍 Bilingual Interface
Full Russian/English support with one‑click switching.
⚡ Automated Scheduling
Retinal (2x/week → daily) and azelaic (from week 3) calculated automatically.
🧴 "My Products"
Mark products you own — only those appear in your calendar.
✅ "Done" Marks
Checkboxes saved in localStorage and synced with Firebase.
🔍 Search & Filter
Find products quickly and filter by "recommended only".
