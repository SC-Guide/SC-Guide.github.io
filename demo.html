💡 Idea & Concept

Skincare Guide was born from a personal need — tracking a complex retinal routine across weeks while managing product choices, daily steps, and bilingual preferences. The project evolved into a full‑scale interactive tool that demonstrates how thoughtful UX can turn a simple tracker into an engaging habit‑forming experience.

Problem Complex retinal schedules, product overload, language switching.
Solution One‑page app with visual calendar, automated logic, and full bilingual support.
Result A portfolio‑ready tool that showcases front‑end, UX, and AI‑driven content strategy.
✨ Key Features

Every feature was designed with a specific user need in mind — from visual navigation to persistent progress tracking.

🐍 Snake‑style Calendar
Days flow in a winding path (boustrophedon) — more engaging than a standard grid, making daily tracking feel like a journey.
🌍 Bilingual Interface
Full Russian/English support with one‑click switching — content strategy meets global accessibility.
⚡ Automated Scheduling
Retinal (2x/week → daily) and azelaic (from week 3) are calculated automatically based on your start date.
📋 Detailed Routine View
Click any day to see exact products for each morning/evening step — no guesswork.
✅ "Done" Marks
Checkboxes saved in localStorage — track your progress across days and months.
🔍 Search & Filter
Quickly find products and filter by "recommended only" — clean, efficient browsing.
