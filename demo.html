01 About the project

Skincare Guide is a personal routine tracker designed to help you plan your daily skincare steps, track retinal usage, and mark completed days. It was built as a portfolio case study to demonstrate content strategy, design systems, responsive layouts, and thoughtful UX.

02 Key Features

🐍

Snake‑style calendar

Days flow in a winding path (boustrophedon) — more engaging than a standard grid.

🌍

Bilingual interface

Full support for Russian and English — switch with one click.

⚡

Automated schedule

Retinal and azelaic acid are generated based on your progress (2x/week → daily).

📋

Detailed routine view

Click a day to see exact products for each morning and evening step.

✅

"Done" marks

Checkboxes are saved in your browser's localStorage — track your progress.

📱

Fully responsive

Works on all devices, from mobile to desktop.
